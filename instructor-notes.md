title: "Instructor Notes"
---

This tutorial follows the environment, simulation, and analysis tutorials in the EIC series. It
assumes learners already have working knowledge of CERN ROOT and Python.

## Before the session

- Ask learners to complete the [Setup](../learners/setup.md) page in advance, in particular the
  installation of `uproot` and `xrootd`. On Google Colab the setup cell takes about 10 minutes to
  run, so it should ideally be done before the live session.
- The lesson streams simulation-campaign files over `xrootd`. Check that the campaign paths in the
  episodes are still accessible before teaching (campaigns roll over and older ones are removed).

## Timing

The lesson is about 55 minutes of teaching plus exercises. Most of the exercises are hands-on
inspection of the simulation and reconstruction output in the notebook environment.
