---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets people who already have some experience with CERN ROOT and Python and who are
starting to work with ePIC simulation and reconstruction output — students, postdocs, and
researchers in the ePIC collaboration who need to understand how the DD4hep and EICrecon output
files are structured and how to read them with `uproot`/`xrootd` and the `edm4hep`/`edm4eic` data
models.
