## Glossary

EIC
:   Electron-Ion Collider

DD4hep
:   The detector-description and Geant4-based simulation toolkit used to propagate particles through
    the ePIC detector geometry and produce simulated hits.

EICrecon
:   The ePIC reconstruction framework, which processes simulated hits into reconstructed quantities
    such as tracks and particles.

edm4hep / edm4eic
:   The PODIO-based event data models used to store the ePIC simulation and reconstruction output.
